## Positive Observations

- The v1→v2 delta closed eleven of fifteen findings substantively rather than cosmetically. AC-1.6's sync manifest replacing mtime, AC-3.3's precedence-ordered exit table, AC-3.5's restore-is-byte-identical oracle, and AC-5.2's "hash authoritative, version reporting-only" are all exactly the right shape, and each states *why* rather than only *what*.
- AC-2.6 → AC-4.1 (hook computes, queue performs one injected read) is a genuinely good answer to v1's F-04: it keeps classification out of the LLM turn without pretending the runtime has a filesystem. NFR-1's per-surface scoping makes the constraint honest instead of aspirational.
- REQ-DIST-04's preamble — hook primary, queue secondary, "No AC in REQ-DIST-04 may be relied on for first adoption" — closes v1's F-05 by construction and is the kind of statement that survives into implementation.
- §0 facts 3–6 are each independently reproducible and correct (verified: no bundle in the 0.10.0 cache; no `pdlc/` segment in the installed layout; `0.9.0` and `0.10.0` ship identical `orchestrate-dev.js`; two versions cached). The plugin-version-is-not-a-drift-signal argument in AC-5.4 is well made.
