- **The `unverified` asymmetry paragraph under AC-3.3 is model spec writing** — it names the two surfaces, states that they optimise for opposite errors, and instructs the test suite to assert both rather than reconcile them. Preserve it verbatim.
- **BL-05 is a well-formed prerequisite.** §0 fact 8 is verified (both `orchestrate-dev.js` and `orchestrate-dev.bundle.js` sit in `.claude/workflows/`, both declaring `meta.name: "orchestrate-dev"`), and the gating logic correctly makes only AC-3.9's *sequencing*, not its existence, depend on the answer.
- **§0 fact 12 checks out** — `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json` declares `"source": "./pdlc"`, which is the real constraint forcing the `dist/` move, and it is cited rather than asserted.
