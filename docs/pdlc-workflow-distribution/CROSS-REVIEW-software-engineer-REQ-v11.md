## Positive Observations

- **The invalidation record is the right fix and it is argued from the row order, not from taste.** Making step 1 a schema-valid AC-2.6 document that lands on the `unresolved` row *below* the `checkEnabled` row — so the fail-closed property is unchanged while the documented escape stays reachable — needs no new queue rule, no schema change and no new queue input, and the record's fields check out one-for-one against AC-2.6 including `generatedBy`. Demoting the unlink to step 2 on the grounds that "an absent file is strictly less informative than an invalidation record and is equally blocking" is the correct ordering argument, and **test (d)** is exactly the assertion that makes the difference from `{}` observable.
- **`readBytes_json` went from a false citation to a reproducible contract in one round.** The heredoc runs as written (`python3 - "$p"` ⇒ `sys.argv[1]`), all four exit codes reproduce, the forbidden pattern is named rather than implied ("must **not** use a bare `except Exception`"), each exit is mapped to the caller's reason, and the two-valued readings elsewhere in the REQ are declared shorthand for exit `2` instead of being left to the reader. Forbidding re-derivation by composing `exists(p)` with a parse step — because the composition is racy — is the kind of rule that survives into review of the implementation.
- **The uid-0 rule is now a predicate rather than an enumeration, which is what made v10's version leak.** "If removing a permission bit is what makes the fixture's expected outcome differ from the all-permissions outcome, this rule applies", with the enumeration demoted to a checklist, is the general form; and naming the write-side failure mode as *red for an environment reason* (rather than false-green) explains why it matters even though it does not lie. F-02 is a bookkeeping error inside the right structure.
- **Superseded dispositions are marked in place rather than rewritten.** Annotating SE v9 F-01 and F-04 with "(Ladder superseded at v11 …)" / "(Extended to nine members at v11 …)" keeps §10 usable as a history instead of a snapshot — a small thing that matters at eleven revisions.
