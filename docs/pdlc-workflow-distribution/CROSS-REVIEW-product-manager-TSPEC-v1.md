| F-01 | High | Local | **AC-2.2 — hook silence — has no assertion anywhere.** AC-2.2 is P0 and is the load-bearing half of US-01: "Silence means everything was verified — never 'could not check'… there is no silent non-green state." §14's 36-row table contains no test whose Then is "the hook emitted nothing", and §1.4's eight floors contain no class for it. `syncedConsumer` (§13.1) is the exact fixture for it and is used by AT-9, AT-11, AT-18a/b and AT-32 — none of which asserts empty stderr. §15.1 hides this: the row `AC-2.1–2.5a, AC-2.8 (warnings, exhaustive) → AT-3, AT-5, AT-11, AT-30` sweeps AC-2.2 into a range of *warning* tests, all of which assert that a message **is** present. ## Positive Observations

- **§0.2 disposes all eight TSPEC-bound FSPEC §10 obligations row by row, with the discharging section named.** This is the cleanest obligation ledger this feature has produced and it makes reviewer verification mechanical rather than archaeological.
- **§1.4's decision to state floors as assertion-count minimums backed by set-equality meta-oracles, rather than line/branch percentages, is right for a black-box bash subject** — and the honesty about *why* ("jest's coverage instrumentation sees none of it and a percentage would be a fabricated number") is exactly the altitude a PM wants.
- **§1.2's load-bearing argument — the oracle must live outside the subject's dependency set — is a genuine product-integrity decision, not an engineering preference.** AT-14 asserting "the record parses" on a tree with no interpreter is the only construction that proves the degraded path actually serves the operator.
- **§6.1's injectability matrix makes every recordable write failure reachable on a root runner**, which means the write-failure suite — the feature's data-loss guard (AC-2.9(4), AC-3.5) — does not quietly evaporate on CI-like environments. The two genuine uid-0 holes (AT-14b, AT-32(a)) are named, not glossed, and the skip strings are the operator-legible record of what went unverified.
- **§4.3 conjunct (a) stated over the row-id *set* rather than a count** correctly closes the vacuous-pass hole in AC-2.9(1); the reasoning about a mis-scoped bash loop is concrete and falsifiable.
- **§10.1's decision to check the `covered-violations` fixture in rather than generate it**, with the stated reason that a generated fixture and a narrowed pattern rot together, protects AC-6.4's anti-widening intent rather than merely its letter. The anchored-gitignore hand-off in §16 is a real hazard caught before it silently zeroed AT-23.
- **§17 states eleven residuals with costs and change conditions and mitigates none by assertion.** Seven of the eleven (R-1 through R-4, R-6 through R-8) are product-acceptable as written. The findings above are about the residuals that are *not* in §17, not about the ones that are.
