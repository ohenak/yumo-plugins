## Positive Observations

- **The batch column is mechanically correct.** I re-derived `batch == max(batch of Deps) + 1` for all **60** tasks (P0-00; T-01…T-38 with T-08 split a/b and no T-39; T-40…T-50; L-01…L-09). Every row matches, the graph is acyclic, ids are unique, and every declared dependency resolves to a real task. 22 batches, as §0 claims.
- **§4's ownership manifest holds under audit.** No physical file is written by two tasks in one batch. The three multi-owner clusters — `pdlc-drift.sh` (6→10), `orchestrate-queue.js` (4, 5, 6, 20) and `dist/**` (3, 4, 5, 6, 21) — are serialised by real `Deps` edges, not prose, and T-50's existence purely to avoid a `queueDriftGate.test.js` collision with T-38 is honest and correctly costed.
- **§6.2's seven-file list is independently reproducible.** Grepping FSPEC §7.5's five literal patterns over the live tree and applying the four exemptions returns exactly the seven paths listed. `CLAUDE.md` and `pdlc/README.md` carry **none** of the five today, which is what makes L-07/L-08's wording constraint a forward obligation rather than a pre-existing violation.
- **P0-00's baseline facts check out at HEAD**: `git ls-files .claude/` returns exactly the four paths; the three sibling hook scripts are all `100644`; `plugin.json` `version` is `0.10.0`; `hooks.json` has one `SessionStart` entry. The gate asserts baseline existence only and correctly asserts nothing about symbols this feature creates.
- **PROPERTIES §12's placement table reconciles task-for-task** with T-41…T-50: every one of the 55 ids has a home, both halves of every split property (CLS-04, BSL-05, MTM-02/-03/-04/-05, NEG-03/-04/-07, RSN-05, SEAM-05) are placed in the task that owns the corresponding suite, and all six `PROP-DET-*` are accounted for across T-41/T-42/T-43. This is the part of the coverage audit that came out clean.
- **TSPEC §12.1's sixteen rows and §12.2's ten rows are cited with the right cardinalities** (T-03, T-04), and T-03's insistence on asserting the **clause id** rather than `ok:false` is exactly the falsifiability standard §12.1 needs.
- **PL-02 is legitimate gap-fill, not scope drift.** TSPEC §1.3 contract 2, §3.2.1's throw, §3.3's `setRowState` self-oracle, §3.4 rules 2–3 and §11.2's line-count check are normative statements with no home in §14's "complete" file table; leaving them unasserted would leave five stated contracts unenforced. The new file adds no dependency-graph complexity. (Its **scope** is the problem, not its existence — F-07.)
- **The uid-0 policy is consistent with TSPEC.** §5's requirement that the four meta-oracles and the ten-row queue floor be "green, **not skipped**, on a uid-0 runner" is achievable given §5.2 tokens 15/16 (which make both `*-artifact-unreadable` row reasons fault-reachable) and the fault-injected twins for AT-16/AT-27/AT-34; the two genuine holes TSPEC names (AT-14b, AT-32(a)) are routed to `itOrSkip` in T-25/T-22 and do not sit under any floor. T-22's explicit "so neither floor skips on a uid-0 runner" is the right instinct.
- **§5's split gate for the RED-terminal batches** is the correct construction: "the new tests fail for the *specified* reason **and** every pre-existing test is still green", with a per-file failing-case count and reason string in the batch report, rather than an unsatisfiable "full suite green".
