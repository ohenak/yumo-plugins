## Positive Observations

- **§2.1's dependent tree is the right product answer to O-9.** REQ v13's failure was 24 undefined cells out of 96; regenerating the axes as a tree rooted at FSPEC §3.2's probes makes the defect *inexpressible* rather than repaired, and turns AC-1.8(i)'s totality into a finite, enumerable claim. This is the single strongest thing in the document.
- **PROP-CLS-02 asserts precedence, not disjointness.** Disjointness over a first-match ladder is vacuous; co-holding fixtures per adjacent pair — including the vacuous-equality trap (`missing` > `in-sync`) and the "no `shasum` plus an absent `pluginPath`" case FSPEC §3.3 records v1 getting wrong — is the falsifiable form, and it protects AC-1.8(ii) at the level the operator experiences.
- **The two biconditionals (PROP-RSN-02, PROP-BSL-02) are correctly identified as load-bearing.** The one-directional reading of AC-1.8(iv) is satisfied by an implementation that nulls the reason on `unknown` rows too, which would silently delete the remediation split AC-1.2 exists to create.
- **PROP-BSL-06 is keyed on evidence rather than on the selected reason.** The vector it protects (`repoRootUnresolved` + `manifestEmpty`) is the *rollout-universal* one, and this is exactly the class of defect where a guard passes every worked example and still writes under a wrong root.
- **PROP-MTM-01's exit-0 conjunct is a product assertion, not a test detail.** Under the rejected reading of AC-2.6 a fully successful sync exits 1 and leaves the queue blocked; §7 correctly identifies itself as the artifact that enforces FSPEC §4.2's reading rather than commenting on it.
- **PROP-MTM-04 disposes the `supersedingState` ambiguity by asserting agreement.** Choosing neither reading, and going red if they ever diverge, is the honest disposition — better than the pick-one it replaces.
- **PROP-NEG-04 enumerates AC-4.1's two exceptions positively**, so a third one is red rather than absorbed — the correct reading of NFR-6's "exactly two exceptions".
- **§1.4's central trade-off is sound in principle.** TSPEC R-3 ("no CI means a slow suite stops being run") is a real product risk for this repo, and "a generated case is a manifest *row*, not a *run*" is the right response — it is made sound rather than assumed by PROP-CLS-06, and P-R-7's stated rule (repack or batch, **never** sample a domain §1.3 rule 2 says to enumerate) is the correct order of preference. F-04 is about the arithmetic, not the reasoning.
- **§1.3's rejection of a property-testing dependency is consistent with the feature's own constraints.** `pdlc/workflows/package.json` carries exactly one devDependency, TSPEC §1.2 rejected bats on that ground, and AC-0.2 excludes `package.json` / lockfile / `node_modules/` from the distributed set — so adding `fast-check` would not reach consumers but *would* re-open a decision this feature just made. P-R-3's mitigation (generators asserted about themselves; `M6_ID_REGEX` imported rather than re-declared; `setRowState` as its own first oracle) is proportionate.

## Recommendation

**Needs revision**

Required to clear:

1. **F-01** — add AC-2.4, AC-3.2, AC-3.7, AC-3.8 to §13, each either traced to a property or listed with the surface that owns it, at the same standard §13 already applies to AC-0.x / AC-2.1 / AC-3.5 / AC-4.1 / AC-6.x.

3. **F-03** — reconcile §0.2's O-11 row and §1.6 with §2.3/§11.1: the skipped leaves are L3/L4, covered by L2/L5.
4. **F-04** — correct §1.4's §9 row (and the "nine hash-present leaves" count), or re-derive the ≈55 ceiling; if the corrected ceiling is materially higher, say so in P-R-7 rather than leaving the R-3 trade-off argued against a number that is no longer the estimate.
5. **F-05** — give D-2's residual an owner that exists, or state in §14 that the per-row-re-probe residual is accepted with **no** owning surface.
6. **F-06** — add `pluginHash` to PROP-NEG-05's perturbation set, or pin the entry's `pluginHash` in §2.3's L9/L10 recipes so the `stale`/`local-edit` discrimination is asserted rather than incidental.

F-08 and F-09 are editorial/disclosure and may be folded into the same revision.
