## Positive Observations

- Every v1 finding was answered by **retraction in place** rather than by an added reconciling clause: §5.1 quotes and withdraws its own descending-search justification, §11.5 defers to §12.4 instead of restating an order, §16.3 withdraws "exactly one" from the terminal test, §4.5 withdraws "each dispatch". That is the right shape for a document whose own review loop failed to converge, and §21.3's retraction table makes it auditable.
- The three fail-open surfaces the charge named are now genuinely closed and each carries a falsifier-bearing test: §10.1's unanimity table makes tier-1 anchor selection **total** (partial ⇒ `UNEVALUABLE`, unequal ⇒ `UNEVALUABLE`, never assembled across roles, no role preferred; E-63/E-64/AT-56, and §21.4's DC-01 row extended to say so); §5.1's single-candidate rule makes a higher round deny the skip *by construction* rather than by a comparison that can be got wrong (AT-57 asserts round 2 "is not read"); §16.3's terminal/approving split removes the false halt without inventing a remediation prompt.
- AT-56…AT-63 all state their own falsifier in the AT text, which is what makes them oracles rather than intentions. AT-42's "*Fails under the four-coordinate episode key, which totals 7*" and AT-61's constant-reason falsifier are the two I would otherwise have had to derive myself.
- The advisory boundary is still honest: §15.8's commit set is now bounded by two `rev-parse` calls with "no author filter, no `--since`, no heuristic" and is **skipped and reported** on failure, and §15.7's "no oracle for emitted bytes exists" survives untouched. The v1.1 additions did not smuggle a halt into an advisory path.
- §20's answered-questions table answers TE Q-01 and TE Q-02 with mechanisms rather than assurances (artifact-set membership "fixed at episode entry", stated in §15.1 itself, not only in the answer).
