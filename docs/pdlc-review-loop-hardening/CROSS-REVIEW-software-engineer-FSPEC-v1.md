## Positive Observations

- **Retraction fidelity is the strongest part of the document, and it checks out.** I verified each load-bearing retraction the prompt flags: AC-4.4 is one hash-equality comparison at both tiers with no history walk at either (§10.1, §10.2); the approval-time hash comes from a **new** `_readFile` at t0 shared with no AC-3.5 measurement, and §7.1 names the retraction explicitly; the recorded sha is the **reviewed document's**, never the cross-review file's own (§7.5, §9.3, with TE-v5 F-03's unimplementability argument reproduced); mode is selected from the kind of prompt being dispatched, with §15.2's walk-through of why v1.4's stickiness leaked; revision terminal is structural completeness **plus** the agent-emitted trailer and explicitly does **not** require progress on the terminal dispatch (§8.4). §21.3's retraction ledger is a genuinely useful reviewing aid and I found no silently reinstated rule.
- **Citations verified at `0655387`** and accurate, including the ones easiest to get wrong: `reviewerRoleSlug`'s `MAP`; `parseVerdict`'s `VALID_VERDICTS` and the unusual sorted-key order `keys[0] !== "high" || keys[1] !== "low" || keys[2] !== "medium"`; the truncated-output branch; `reviewLoop`'s `if (iteration > 5)`; the dead `const postmortemPath = \`docs/{feature}/POSTMORTEM-${phaseId}-{feature}.md\`` in `checkConverged` (distinct from `reviewLoop`'s correctly-substituted one, which the FSPEC does not confuse it with); the `"⏭"` marker on Phase D; `DOD_MAX_ITERATIONS = 3`; `stripModuleSyntax`'s exact replace rule; `RT_IO_MODEL = "haiku"`; `rtDevInjections`'s nine entries and its omission of `_writeFile` despite `rtWriteFile` being defined directly above; `QUEUE_ENTRY`'s `_writeFile: rtWriteFile`; `DEV_META` carrying no `inputs` array; `updateQueueStatus`'s `return markdown; // feature row not found`; `selectNextPending`'s no-candidate reason string; `listAllFiles` / `WALK_SKIP_DIRS` in `document-oracles.mjs`. No bare `file:line` citation anywhere, as claimed.
- **§17.6 gets the generated-artifact tier right** — `pdlc/workflows/dist/` rebuilt and committed with its sources, `.claude/workflows/` never committed and asserted only through `sync-workflows.sh --check`. This is the exact error SE-v1 F-01 caught on the REQ and it is not repeated.
- **Scope discipline holds.** D-RLH-01…05 are specified nowhere; §15.1 and §15.4 name D-RLH-05 and D-RLH-04 only as exclusion rationale, which is correct. `docs/requirements/traceability-matrix.md` **pre-exists on `main`** (added in #19) and this branch only appended 13 lines to it — additive maintenance of an existing repo artifact, not unrequested scope, and not a finding.
- §11.1's rejection of env var / marker file / config key for `forcePhases`, grounded in the absence of `process` and in `DEV_ENTRY`'s already-object-tolerant `args` test, is the right call for the right measured reason. §7.2's rejection of an agent-relayed `shasum` on narration grounds, and §6.3's refusal to reuse `recoverVerdict` on the approval path, are both correct C-5 readings.
- §18's 62-row edge-case table and §19's 55 acceptance tests are unusually complete for an FSPEC; F-02 is notable precisely because it is one of very few reachable states the table misses.

## Recommendation

**Needs revision**

Two High findings must close before TSPEC: F-01 (pick one gate ordering — §12.4's is the one AC-2.3b and E-26 require) and F-02 (state the tier-1 `recordedHash` source and the fail-closed outcome when a round's two anchors are partial or unequal, with an §18.2 row). The four Mediums are all correctable in place: F-03 correct the git claim and add the precedent disposition; F-04 add the three `build-runtime.mjs`/export edits to §17.3 plus `_git` on the queue's `main()`; F-05 restate AT-19 so it is green on a correct bundle and extend its seam list; F-06 state an early-exit or a read budget for the descending approval search.
