| F-01 | High | Local | **AC-3.5a's new predicate is saturated in the *opposite* direction for the edit-shaped dispatches AC-3.6/AC-3.2b deliberately bring into AC-3's scope: every feedback-addressing dispatch scores "no progress", so three of them halt the phase.** Progress is now defined as a *strict increase in the count of top-level sections satisfying the AC-3.4 completeness criterion*. On a revision dispatch — a round-2+ author agent addressing cross-review findings, or an AC-3.6 remediation agent — the document is **already structurally complete before the dispatch**, so the count is already at its maximum and cannot strictly increase no matter how correct the edit. Then AC-3.5b counts a failed script-owned attempt and **re-dispatches with the resume prompt** (a prompt that names "the first unwritten section" — there is none), and AC-3.5c halts at 3. AC-3.3 makes this concrete rather than hypothetical: a structurally complete document on disk means the dispatch "is a first attempt", so the wrapper engages with a pre-measurement equal to its post-measurement. Nothing in AC-3.5 scopes the wrapper to greenfield authoring, and the document pushes the other way — AC-3.6 binds review/remediation agents to the same budget and AC-3.1a says it "applies identically under AC-3.6", AC-3.2b extends the commit cadence to "**every** agent bound by AC-3.6". So the two available readings ("the wrapper covers only the initial authoring dispatch" vs "the wrapper covers every dispatch that writes the artifact") give opposite terminal statuses for the single most common dispatch in this pipeline — this REQ has itself had three authoring dispatches in one phase, two of them revisions — and the second reading makes the mechanism halt every phase at round 3. A test writer cannot write either the happy-path revision test or the negative "revision must not halt" test. ## Recommendation

**Needs revision**

Exactly what must change:

1. **F-01** — state which dispatches the dispatch-and-verify wrapper wraps, and give edit-shaped dispatches on an already-complete artifact a progress predicate that can change (or an explicit exemption). As written, every revision round scores no progress and halts at 3.
2. **F-02** — reconcile `MAX_AUTHORING_DISPATCHES` with AC-1.6's five-round budget: re-scope it, or raise and re-derive it so a phase converging at the last permitted round does not trip it.
3. **F-03** — either name an observable oracle for AC-3.1a's emitted-byte bound or record it as agent-directed and script-unverifiable, with AC-3.5 named as the compensating measurable control. Do not leave AC-3.1a held to a weaker standard than AC-1.4 without saying so.

F-04 and F-05 are one clause each inside AC-3.5 and can ride along with F-01. All five findings are inside AC-3; every other v1.2 addition (AC-1.4a, AC-2.3b, AC-4.2b/c, AC-4.6a, AC-4.7a, §4a A-7/A-8, R-8, O-21, O-22) is approved from a testing lens.
