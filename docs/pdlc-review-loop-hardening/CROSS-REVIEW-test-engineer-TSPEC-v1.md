## Positive Observations

- **§5.0 `scanLines` as the one scanner.** Specifying fence handling once, as a function, with the three properties the callers depend on stated explicitly — and with the deliberate refusal to let the exclusion empty a section body (the v1.4 false-halt regression) — is the right shape and removes an entire class of per-site divergence.
- **§8.2's fixture pinning is a real falsifier, not a description.** Naming `quoted-verdict.md` as the **nested** four-in-three form, with the explicit note that a three-in-three fixture "would pass under the wrong implementation", is exactly the discipline that makes AT-65 a test rather than an intention. The surrogate-pair digest vector does the same job for `utf8Bytes`.
- **§4.2's asymmetry between `dir_missing` and the other three `ListFailure` values** is argued from the failure mode ("H-1's failure mode reintroduced through the error path") rather than asserted, and it is the correct fail-closed direction.
- **§4.8 and T-Q-03 record that `MAX_AUTHORING_WRITE_BYTES` has no oracle** instead of implying one. A constant documented as advisory is worth more than a constant that looks enforced.
- **§3.7's argument for `sha256Hex` not being a seam** — that a seam buys a capability, and a double could return a hash production never computes — is a testability argument, and the right one.
- **T-Q-01 is carried rather than silently widened.** Adding `PR` to the phase set would have quietly broken AT-29's verbatim string; declining to take a REQ-altitude decision inside a TSPEC is correct.
- **§5.5's rebase invariance** is stated with its falsifier (Phase DOD rewrites every sha) rather than as a preference, and §5.3's "copy, never recompute" closes the corresponding harvest-time laundering path that AT-15 exists to catch.
