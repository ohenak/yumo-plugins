## The AT-64 predicate — measured judgement

**The author is right and I was wrong.** I measured the new predicate against the real signature of `main()` in `pdlc/workflows/orchestrate-dev.js` at HEAD. All sixteen `_`-prefixed parameters classify, and each classification is correct:

- **Wired (9)** — `_agent`, `_parallel`, `_pipeline`, `_phase`, `_log`, `_checkFile`, `_readFile`, `_checkCi`, `_mergeWorktree`; matches `rtDevInjections`'s nine entries exactly.
- **E-1 (2)** — `_phaseDodEnabled: phaseDodEnabled = PHASE_DOD_ENABLED`, `_phasePubEnabled: phasePubEnabled = PHASE_PUB_ENABLED`; both constants are `= true` at module level, non-function literals. ✔
- **E-2 (2)** — `_now,` and `_sleep,` are declared with no initialiser at all. ✔ (and `raisePrAndVerifyCi` does default them, `_now = () => Date.now()`, `_sleep = sleep`, as the row claims).
- **E-3 (3)** — `rebaseOntoDefault({ feature, _agent = agent, _log = log })`, `dodVerifyLoop({ feature, maxIterations, _agent = agent, _log = log })`, `raisePrAndVerifyCi({ feature, _agent = agent, _checkCi, _log, _now, _sleep, … })`. All three are module-declared functions whose destructured parameter list contains `_agent`. ✔ These are precisely the three my v1 F-04 measured as satisfied by nothing.

The **declining** of my suggested predicate is also correct, and I verified the counter-measurement rather than accepting it. "Default is a locally-defined function of this module" exempts, at HEAD, `_checkFile = checkFileNonEmpty`, `_readFile = defaultReadFile`, `_mergeWorktree = mergeWorktree` and `_checkCi = checkPrCi` — four parameters that are simultaneously *in* `rtDevInjections`, i.e. four genuine capability seams, and after this feature `defaultListFiles` / `defaultGit` / `defaultAppendFile` / `defaultRecordHalt` join them. My predicate would have exempted the entire wired set. It was wrong; E-3's `_agent`-parameter discriminator is the right narrowing, and I confirmed none of the wired defaults declares an `_agent` parameter (`checkPrCi` and `mergeWorktree` take `{ execFn }`, `checkFileNonEmpty` takes `{ fsMod }`, `defaultReadFile` takes `path`).

**Does it still red when a genuine seam is dropped?** Yes for E-1 and E-3 — a dropped `_git` (default `defaultGit`, a function, not `_agent`-parametered) falls in no class and the test names it. The one hole is E-2, filed as N-02: any new seam declared without an initialiser is silently exempt, and no anti-rot clause covers that form. That is a one-conjunct fix, not a defect in the design of the predicate.

## Positive Observations

- **The two root-cause deletions are the right shape.** Removing the trailer conjunct from the greenfield path (rather than adding "these classes are exempt from the trailer") and demoting the POSTMORTEM gate to a per-path query (rather than adding an exemption list) both leave *one* rule where a reconciling clause would have left two that must be kept in sync with §7.4's amendment list. This is lesson R-5 applied rather than cited.
- **§8.5's E-3 argument is a testability argument.** "A future seam can only be exempted by acquiring an `_agent` parameter, which is a change to its signature that a reviewer sees" is the correct standard for a predicate that replaces a hand-list — it names the *observable* that makes exemption expensive. N-02 is that argument being true of two of the three forms rather than three.
- **Declining my F-04 suggestion on measured evidence, and saying so.** §1.4's DC-02 paragraph records that two v1.1 measurements *overturned* a reviewer's premise. A revision that pushes back with numbers is worth more than one that complies.
- **§5.3's four-branch table refuses to repair.** "They deliberately do not overwrite, delete or reconcile the existing anchors — a 'repair' here would launder exactly the ambiguity that should be visible" is the right fail-closed instinct, and it is argued from the failure mode rather than asserted.
- **§6.2 row 9a exists.** Recording the greenfield trailer absence as a deliberate non-event, rather than leaving it out of the table, is what makes the E-catalogue readable as *total* rather than merely long.
- **T-Q-05 closed by measurement, not by argument.** 140,096 B against 92,525 B, with the observation that the larger artifact already contains exactly this union and has shipped — that closes the question in a way no amount of reasoning about size budgets would have.
- **The REQ v1.6 / FSPEC v1.6 amendment is carried honestly.** The `PR` widening is one token, the three consumer sites are enumerated in all three changelogs, and §5.7 makes the point that the rejection message and the catalogue are one array so they cannot desynchronise — with AT-29's verbatim assertion as the guard for both halves. I verified the FSPEC diff touches only §10.7, §11.1 and §11.3 as claimed.
