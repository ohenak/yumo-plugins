## Decision

**The four decisions hold; the document's account of its evidence does not.** DEC-A6-01, DEC-A6-02, DEC-A6-03 and DEC-A6-04 are each still the right call against TSPEC v1.6, and each is now *better* supported than it was against v1.5: the O-8 row agrees with DEC-A6-02 instead of contradicting it, the ref-naming rejection has the two-wave oracle it lacked, the `0` affordance has the behaviour arm it lacked, and the engine-channel expectation has an owner in §5.1. I re-checked the passages that could have been contradicted by the v1.6 edits and were not — §5.5's mutation fixtures still count a capture-unique verb (`commit-tree === 1`, `TSPEC:102`, `:1151`), so DEC-A6-01's double-sizing consequence is untouched; the `consumesAttempt: true` re-entry argument at DECISIONS:247-248 is unaffected by v1.6's per-attempt `seamBudgetMinutes` re-grounding of AT-02-7, which DECISIONS never leaned on.

Confirmation is therefore **not** clean. Four passages assert an upstream state that no longer exists, two of them in the form "no test falsifies this" about regressions that are now falsified upstream. Under this lens that is a High: a coverage claim that is wrong in the *pessimistic* direction still misroutes work, and the `0`-collapse bullet ends in an instruction-shaped sentence ("Until it lands, a future simplification … passes the suite") that a PROPERTIES author would act on by writing an oracle that already exists, or by treating a retired risk as live.

## Consequences

- The remedy is subtractive and mechanical. Each passage becomes shorter: the routed erratum is replaced by the landed citation. F-01's bullet keeps its behavioural spec (`0` on a red wave ⇒ escalate, zero `_agent` calls, sixth row present at zero) and swaps "Nothing anywhere exercises" for a pointer at §5.2's fixture; F-02's paragraph keeps the rejection reasoning and swaps "stated but not falsifiable" for §4.5's new clause plus §5.2's set-equality; F-03's paragraph drops the stale quotation and the disagreement it described, keeping the FSPEC BR-8 reading; F-04's bullet swaps "no AT covers it yet" for §5.1's ownership row. No decision text changes, no option is re-opened, and no new oracle is specified in this document.
- **The set-equality discipline this document itself teaches applies to it.** The whole-feature consequence warns that a literal transcribed at a dozen sites goes stale by partial edit, with "a failure reason the record cannot predict". A DECISIONS entry that transcribes upstream text and upstream coverage status is the same shape of surface, and this cascade is the failure firing on the record rather than on the code. Worth one sentence in LEARNINGS at harvest: an entry whose value is "here is the gap upstream still has" is a transcription with an expiry date, and the erratum that closes the gap should close the entry in the same wave.
- Nothing here blocks PLAN. If the four edits land in one pass the document is confirmed; none of them touches the decision lines a PLAN task would be derived from.
