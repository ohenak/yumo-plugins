## Positive Observations

- **The routing call in v5's F-01 was the right one and it paid off.** Rather than reinterpreting AT-1.3 TSPEC-side, §6.1 erratum 9 sent the wording upstream, and FSPEC v0.7 landed a *better* clause than the one proposed — keyed to sink membership at run time rather than to `SKIP_INVENTORY` membership, which avoids failing correct skips whose names the spec-derived inventory deliberately does not close over (`helpers/skipSink.js:37`–`:46`). The remaining work is transcription, not re-litigation.
- **The rejected alternative is costed, not waved off.** §5.5 now states why pinning the gate runner to non-root is cheaper and still wrong — a green AT-1.3 that is an artefact of runner configuration the oracles cannot see. That is the paragraph I asked for in v5's Q-01, and it answers it.
- **The comparator's silence is named precisely.** §5.5 says `validateSkipRecords` is "silent, not green-with-evidence" for a bare `it.skip`, and pre-empts the cheapest false-green (`pendingCount === sinkRecords.length`). That is the correct reading of `helpers/skipSink.js:155`–`:203`, and it is what makes F-02/F-03 fixable by editing the oracle's domain and placement rather than rethinking the approach.
- **Scope rule 4 closed the orphan-oracle hole properly.** Reachability from test-module and config roots, closing over helper-to-helper edges, kills the mutually-importing-pair loophole without widening the exception list; I re-derived it over the simulated post-sweep tree and `helpers/skipSink.js` stays reachable via `skipSinkTransport.test.js`.

## Recommendation

**Needs revision** — three High findings, all in §5.5/§6.1 and all mechanical. The upstream erratum this TSPEC raised has been **accepted** (FSPEC v0.7), so §5.5 must be re-derived from the landed AT-1.3 wording (F-01); with that wording the left set becomes the swept surface, which is also what stops the oracle reding forever against `guardMatrix.test.js`'s pre-existing pending rows (F-02); and the join needs a stated, constructible place to run given that the sink is deleted in `globalTeardown` and the run's `--json` does not exist mid-run (F-03). Nothing else in the diff is contested.
