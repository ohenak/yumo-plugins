## Positive Observations

- **Every pinned literal I could re-derive is exact.** `pdlc/workflows/dist/` holds precisely L-1's five entries; `pdlc/hooks/hooks.json` registers precisely L-4's five entries in the stated event/matcher/script shape, with two sibling `SessionStart` entries — which is what makes L-4's set-equality (and E-14) a real guard rather than a restatement; `ls pdlc/workflows/__tests__/*.test.js | wc -l` is **119**; all 21 M-8 modules plus `runtimeProvenanceWiring.test.js` exist by the exact names L-5 lists, so 119 − 22 = 97 is arithmetic over verified members, not an estimate; `pdlc/skills/*/SKILL.md` is **15**.
- **L-7's check names are byte-exact against the workflows**, including the rendered/authored distinction the oracle enforces (`Generated artifacts are in sync`, not "…artifacts in sync"; `Fixture machine (install/upgrade, launcher, container, two-repo)`). A test author can transcribe them without re-deriving.
- **L-3 is a faithful transcription** of the baseline's AC-1.2 command (identical modulo indentation and the trailing `# AC-1.2 term set` comment), and L-2's exclusion argument holds under check: `pdlc/engine/__tests__/ci-arrangement.test.js` matches **none** of the seven terms, which is exactly why BR-SWEEP-5 treats it as a read-not-grep dependent.
- **BR-VER-1's handshake triple verifies end to end**: `pdlc/engine/package.json:18` declares `^0.23.0`, `pdlc/.claude-plugin/plugin.json:4` is `0.23.1`, `satisfiesRange` exists in `pdlc/engine/lib/handshake.mjs`, and `docs/completed/pdlc-engine-distribution/EVIDENCE-ENGINE-V0.2.0.md` carries the published `pluginCompat: ^0.23.0`. E-19 is the right edge case and it is stated positively.
- **Set-equality is used as the default completeness shape** (L-1, L-2, L-4, L-7, L-10, AT-1.7, AT-5.2 field sets) rather than containment, and E-11/E-12/E-13 pre-refuse the three obvious ways an implementer would loosen it. This is the single most useful property of the document for test authors.
- **E-23** (a suite that ran zero tests and exited 0 is not a green start) is precisely the vacuous-green guard this kind of sweep usually lacks.
- **BR-CLN-3's "deletes nothing at all"** makes the refusal path binary and therefore cheap to test; AT-4.3's byte-identity conjunct is the right positive pairing for a refusal assertion.

## Recommendation

**Needs revision** — three High findings. Two are oracle-shape defects that would ship a gate
unable to fail (F-03) or unable to pass (F-02); one is a coverage gap that makes the sweep's own
per-commit green rule unachievable at its first commit (F-01). None requires re-architecting the
specification: F-01 extends one commit class and one business rule, F-02 needs an enumeration
(and an upstream REQ correction, routed as an erratum), F-03 needs one positive conjunct added to
an existing test. The literals, the commit ordering and the set-equality discipline are in good
shape and should survive the revision untouched.
