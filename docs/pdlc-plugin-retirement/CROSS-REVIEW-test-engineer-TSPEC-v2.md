## Recommendation

**Needs revision**

Two High findings, both narrow and both inside sections this revision changed. F-01 is the one
that matters: the clause-1 fix landed one level too shallow, and as written the sweep's single
behavioural-equivalence gate cannot detect a lost `engine.outcomes` or `engine.dispatches` key —
excluded from set-equality by the new rule and from value comparison by the old one. F-02 is a
scope contradiction between §4.4 and TT-3 that, taken at TT-3's wording, deletes mode-bit
coverage of three surviving hook scripts with no replacement.

Neither touches the document's architecture, its class map, or its erratum routing, all of which
have improved markedly this round. The revision needed is two sentences in §4.5 clause 1(b), one
`it.each` in §5.2 TT-3, and the four Medium/Low corrections in §3.2, §2.7 and the two AT-3.3
citations. Every other v1 finding is resolved and verified against the tree.
