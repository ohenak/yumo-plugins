## Positive Observations

- **The F-01 fix from v3 landed as a widening, not a patch, and says so.** §4.4 does not merely append `check-req-size.sh` to a list; it states that `FIVE_SCRIPTS` was never coextensive with AC-3.3's hook set, names the omission, and characterises the re-home as covering "`FIVE_SCRIPTS`'s three survivors plus `check-req-size.sh` (which ships today with no mode-bit oracle at all) and the new `cleanup-consumer-workflows.sh`". That is the difference between fixing a count and fixing the reasoning that produced the count — the next reader cannot re-derive the old wrong set from the new text. Verified against HEAD: `check-req-size.sh` is tracked `100755` and registered as the second `PostToolUse` command at `pdlc/hooks/hooks.json:24`.
- **TT-3 now answers v3's Q-01 in the row itself.** The row states the set-equality is **one-directional** (tracked-executable set, not the `hooks.json` manifest set) and explains why `cleanup-consumer-workflows.sh` — never a registered hook, per §3.2 — would otherwise force AC-1.7 to carve it back out. That was an open question about redundant design, and it came back as a scoping sentence rather than a defence, which is the right shape.
- **§5.2's AT-3.3 row got weaker claims and stronger citations at the same time.** The v0.3 text asserted "neither covered today" over two hooks; v0.4 withdraws that for `check-scope-field.sh`, credits `PROP-COMPAT-04`'s existing exit-`0` assertion, and reduces the new work to strengthening containment into `PROP-COMPAT-06`'s parsed shape. Both citations check out (`hookCompatibility.test.js:100`, `:332`). Narrowing your own claim when the tree contradicts it is the behaviour that makes the rest of the document's citations trustworthy.
- **§5.5's new assertion is written in the positive direction on purpose, and says why.** "`helpers/freshClone.js` is imported by name — the positive direction, so that sweeping it as collateral reds rather than passing vacuously" is exactly the absence-only-oracle trap avoided out loud. F-01 is about the universal's scope, not its direction; the direction is right, and the fix preserves it.
- **TT-1b splits assertable from unassertable rather than dropping the row.** The new row asserts the constructible arm (unreadable target ⇒ exit exactly `4` plus a diagnostic naming the failing path on stderr) and states plainly that the partial-`rm` arm stays oracle-free because it cannot be constructed without faking the removal primitive. Row 4b's exit status is now reached by a test instead of being covered only by prose, and the honest gap is named where an implementer will read it.
