## Positive Observations

- **The widening was done at the rule's altitude, not patched row by row.** v8 asked for a wider table; v0.9 restated the membership rule first, answered Q-01 in the document rather than in a reply, propagated the same wording into erratum 10's requested FSPEC limb, and only then enumerated. That ordering is why F-01 is a one-module gap rather than a fresh disagreement about scope.
- **The green/red child split is the right shape, and it fixed a real unsatisfiability.** A single file list containing the falsifier could never come out empty; two invocations with separately transcribed lists give the pass reading and the falsifiability construction each their own oracle, and the red child's expectation names the fixture's leaf title rather than merely expecting non-empty output.
- **Part 3 now meets the standard it was asking of everyone else.** A source scan that could silently stop matching was the weakest link in the carve-out; runtime-assembled tokens plus a known-marker positive plus a fixture-hit positive means the guard reds when it breaks instead of passing quietly.
- **Every code claim I spot-checked in the delta holds at the tree**: `orchestrateDevSkill.test.js:93`'s exact `toContain` argument, the absence of pending markers in the three newly tabulated class-7/9/11 modules, `documentOracles.test.js`'s registered `itOrSkip` sites, `consolidationBuild.test.js`'s `describe.skip` occurrences being comment-text only (one match, in the header block), and `consolidationHookParity.test.js`'s four ternary sites and their two capability predicates.
