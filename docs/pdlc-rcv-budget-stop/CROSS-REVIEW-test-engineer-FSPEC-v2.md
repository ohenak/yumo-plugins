## Positive Observations

- **Every one of the five must-change items was answered by changing the observable, not by rephrasing it.** F-01 and F-04 were the two where the FSPEC had *asserted* a non-discriminating oracle; both are now replaced with pairs of conjuncts that state explicitly why the weaker form cannot substitute (`"a count-based oracle cannot substitute — both values are 3 after this ship"`, `"the third alone would not"`). Writing the refutation into the document is what stops the weaker form being reintroduced by a later editor who does not have this review.
- **F-02 was closed by removing the state, not dispositioning it.** B-CLR-2a could have been an accepted-cost paragraph; instead the `D > E` case is routed to the fresh-window branch so that the invariant *one clearance yields at least one round* holds on **every** branch with no exceptions. §12(a) now proves that invariant branch by branch with two different arguments, and says which argument applies where. That is the shape of an answer a successor feature can inherit.
- **F-03's disposition argues from BR-10/BR-12 rather than from convenience.** "Honouring the pre-feature marker would mean minting an `H` the region never recorded" is a real reason to accept the two-clearance cost, and it is the reason that generalises. E-1b also names the file on disk, so the migration case is checkable rather than hypothetical.
- **AT-CLR-02a's `Then` names its own falsifying condition.** *"the two conjuncts that fail if the branch resumes instead"* tells a maintainer what the row is for, which is exactly what stops a future weakening — and is why F-09 matters enough to file: that sentence is only true if the conjuncts cannot fail for an unrelated reason.
- **§12(f) chose the golden file over the derived expectation, and said why.** "Would re-implement the production transform in the test and could not fail" is the correct reason, stated at the altitude where PROPERTIES inherits it.
- **I re-derived the arithmetic in all five new/rewritten AT rows** (AT-BUD-03a/03b, AT-CLR-02, AT-CLR-02a, AT-CLR-04, AT-CLR-08, AT-REG-07, AT-RPT-06/07) rather than trusting the prose. Every window bound, resolved start and appended value is correct against §5.2 and §6.1. §13.1's traceability was updated for all four new rows with no orphans.

**Exactly what must change:**

1. **F-09 (Medium)** — add one clause to §11.4 fixing the dispatch outcome for clearance rows: in every row that grants a window, the granted window's dispatched rounds **converge before the window's last round**, unless the row states otherwise. Without it, AT-CLR-02a's discriminating conjunct (*no second `HALT-REASON:`, marker not stripped*) false-reds on a correct implementation whenever the granted window is allowed to run to exhaustion, and AT-CLR-04's *"nothing appended on either entry"* is false one round after the point v1 F-05 addressed. This is the one item I would hold a round for: AT-CLR-02a **is** F-02's fix, and its oracle has to be robust or F-02 is not actually closed in a testable sense.
2. **F-08 (Low)** — restate AT-REG-07's "equivalent well-formed" as equivalence over the **gate-relevant state** (same truth of `A < H`, same resolved `W`, same resolved `D`) instead of *same counts*, so the `H − A ∉ {0, 1}` member has a constructible pair. Need not gate the round on its own.
