## Positive Observations

- The AC-3.2 split is the right correction rather than a wording patch: a per-document catalogue and a corpus-level catalogue are two different fixture shapes, and stating "two set-equality tests, one per catalogue" tells the test author how many completeness tests exist before they read TSPEC. The follow-on edits to AC-4.1 and AC-4.2 kept the vocabulary consistent, so `RSN-EMPTY` and `RSN-UNLISTABLE` are reachable from every AC that can produce them.
- C-3/O-7's rewrite goes further than the finding asked: it does not just stop citing DEC-CONS-05 as authority for a shared definition, it names what the decision actually shipped and disowns the agreement oracle explicitly ("No agreement test against the `SessionStart` hook is owed"). That closes the door on a downstream reviewer re-proposing the red-on-correct-code test.
- AC-1.2's added scoping clause pairs with AC-4.3's existing one, so both places where this REQ asks for a cross-run comparison now say the comparison happens under fixtures. Round-window and verdict comparisons are the classic live-run false-red in this pipeline; the REQ now closes both.
- C-1's shift from prose ("every dispatch the pipeline tags as an authoring dispatch") to the literal `dispatchKind: "authoring"` token plus its HEAD sites makes the oracle greppable — a reviewer can re-derive the set in one command, which is how the taxonomy claim was checked for this review.
