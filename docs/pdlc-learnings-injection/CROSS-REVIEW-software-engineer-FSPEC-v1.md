| F-01 | High | Local | BR-5's "the count of contributing documents **equals** the threshold exactly" is falsified by BR-6's total bound on the real corpus, so the two rules contradict each other. §4.1 declares `maxDocuments: 5`, `maxBytesPerDocument: 6,000`, `maxTotalBytes: 20,000` (REQ:220-223): 5 × 6,000 = 30,000 > 20,000, so the total bound binds before the count bound whenever documents are large. Measured at HEAD over the 89-document corpus (sections 1–5, Approval Record excluded per BR-6): mean injectable material 13,278 bytes, max 41,180, and **87 of 89 documents exceed `maxBytesPerDocument` on their own**. Every selected document is therefore bounded to ~6,000 bytes and `maxTotalBytes` admits at most 3 of them — the contributing count is 3, never 5, for any realistic corpus. AT-07 asserts counts of `1, maxDocuments, maxDocuments` for corpora of 1/5/50 and can only pass on fixtures made artificially small, which is a fixture tuned to the assertion rather than to the behaviour. Restate BR-5 as "at most `maxDocuments`, and exactly `maxDocuments` only where the total bound does not bind first", requalify AT-07 to assert the count under both regimes, and record the measurement as evidence for REQ O-1. | BR-5, BR-6, AT-07 |
