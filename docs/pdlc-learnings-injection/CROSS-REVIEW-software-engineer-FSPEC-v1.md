## Positive Observations

- BR-4's measurement is exact and reproduces at HEAD: 9 of 9 corpus documents in `yumo-plugins` and 78 of 80 in `regime-ledger` carry a `Date Completed` row (89 / 87 total), and the two exceptions named in the table — `docs/completed/43-wheel-rationale-contract/` and `docs/completed/34-postgres-audit-repository/` — are exactly the two documents that lack it. Free-text suffixes occur as claimed. This is the strongest kind of REQ obligation discharge: a measured basis a reviewer can re-derive in one command.
- F-O-4's blocker is verified: the engine vendors only `orchestrate-dev.js` and `orchestrate-queue.js` (`pdlc/engine/scripts/prepack.mjs:20`), so import of the corpus predicate genuinely is unavailable and literal restatement plus a pinning test is the right shape.
- BR-1 consumes the pipeline's existing classification rather than restating a list, and that classification really exists and really is already a prompt-composition seam: `dispatchKind === "authoring"` at `orchestrate-dev.js:8886`, with creator (`:13515`), optimizer (`:7659-7663`) and both erratum dispatches (`:12821`, `:12915`) on the authoring side and review (`:12653`, `:12980`) and harvest (`:14731`) off it. The feature has a real place to sit.
- BR-12's corpus-state table is total, and the "worst case is inject nothing, record why" framing means no corpus state can halt a run. The pairing of a corpus-level catalogue with per-document reasons — and the rule that a corpus-level outcome still leaves BR-8's rows present-and-empty — closes the gap where "nothing selected" and "nothing recorded" look alike.
- AC-6.2's recorded pre-feature baseline, committed and never regenerated by the branch under test, is the right defence against an implementation echo in the byte-identity tests, and F-O-5 carries the capture mechanics forward rather than leaving them implied.
- The DC-01 closure discipline is applied per catalogue rather than once — BR-8's fields, BR-9's two catalogues, BR-10's members each get their own set-equality test (AT-17/19/20/22). That is the correct granularity.

## Recommendation

**Needs revision**

Four High findings gate. In priority order: F-01 and F-02 are internal contradictions that the
measured corpus makes certain rather than hypothetical — the declared bounds cannot deliver
`maxDocuments` documents, and a real document at HEAD would occupy a slot while injecting nothing.
F-03 asks BR-14 to be redrawn against the config-reader semantics the repository already ships, so
the malformed state is decidable. F-04 needs the REQ and the FSPEC to stop disagreeing about
AC-2.2, either by adopting the `(basename, path)` tiebreak or by landing the erratum.

The Medium findings (F-05..F-09) are all resolvable inside the same revision and none of them
require re-architecting the flow. The document's overall shape — a pure selection function over
repository state, fail-open under every corpus state, closed catalogues with per-catalogue
completeness tests — is right, and the measurement work behind BR-4 sets the bar for how a REQ
obligation should be discharged.
