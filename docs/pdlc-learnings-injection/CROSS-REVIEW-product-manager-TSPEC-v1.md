## Positive Observations

- **Every grounded premise I checked holds.** P-1 (`MODULE_NAMES = ["orchestrate-dev.js", "orchestrate-queue.js"]`, `pdlc/engine/scripts/prepack.mjs:20`), P-2's four `dispatchKind: "authoring"` sites (`orchestrate-dev.js:7663`, `:12821`, `:12915`, `:13515` — and `grep '"authoring"'` shows no fifth), P-3 (`dispatchAndVerify`'s seam list at `:8862-8878`, prompt composition at `:8978`; `reviewLoop`'s `wrapped` does reach it, `:7342-7358`), P-4 (`LS_FILES_ARGV`, `consolidate-learnings.js:1338-1346`), P-7/P-8 (`defaultGit:11658-11676`, `defaultReadFile:11513-11519` returns `null`, `rtReadFile` rethrows an exhausted probe), P-10 (`...(advisory ? { advisory } : {})`, `:15167`), P-11/P-12 (`parseAdvisoryConfig:1971-2010`, `ADVISORY_DEFAULTS.enabled === false` at `:1944`). P-5 I re-ran: the pinned argv yields exactly 9 documents, all 9 opening `# LEARNINGS — {feature}` and all 9 carrying a bare-ISO `Date Completed` row. A reviewer can check this document's premises rather than trust them, which is what §Grounded premises promised.
- **AC-4.1 and AC-5.1a are made structural rather than tested.** §A.2 property 3 (empty string suffix, never a marker or header) and §I.4's `buildLearningsInjector → null` mean an empty corpus, an unlistable corpus and an off run produce a character-identical prompt by construction. Making a fail-open guarantee unfalsifiable-by-construction and *then* still testing it (§T.3) is the right order.
- **The AC-2.6 three-way outcome falls out of the predicate instead of a rule of our own.** §A.3's observation that `:(glob)docs/*/…` does not cross `/` gives `docs/discarded/{p}/…` excluded, `docs/discarded/LEARNINGS-*.md` included and `docs/completed/{p}/…` included — exactly REQ AC-2.6's three clauses, with no exclusion list to drift. This is the strongest form of C-3's "reuse the shipped predicate" available given P-1.
- **The pin is a real agreement oracle.** §I.1 drives `enumerateCorpus(fakeGit)` and compares the observed argv, rather than transcribing the pathspec a third time — so a change on either side reds the test. That closes REQ O-7 as O-7 asked.
- **AC-4.4 is honoured precisely.** §I.2's non-negative-integer validation (rather than the sibling's positive-integer rule) is what makes `0` an admits-nothing *valid* configuration instead of an invalid key silently restored to its default. That is an easy detail to get wrong by copying the sibling, and it was not.
- **Upstream defects are routed, not absorbed.** ERR-1/ERR-2/ERR-3 each name a specific FSPEC claim, give the contradicting evidence at HEAD, and leave the fix with the owning document — including ERR-3, which observes that BR-15's expected set cannot hold on the instrument BR-15 names. Reporting a defect in an oracle you are about to be measured by is the behaviour the pipeline wants.
- **The count-bound gap REQ O-8 named is closed with a named fixture.** `COUNT-BINDING` (8 documents, one 200-byte section each, `maxDocuments: 3`) plus its `BYTES-BINDING` mirror means each bound is exercised where it binds *and* asserted not to bind where the other does.

## Recommendation

**Needs revision** — one High finding (F-01).

The technical shape is sound from a product lens: it traces to the requirements, it does not invent product behaviour, and its fail-open story is stronger than REQ asked for. The blocker is a single product decision that this document settles on its own authority.

Exactly what to change:

1. **F-01 (High)** — Do not close the "`enabled` defaults true only within a present section" decision in the rejected-alternatives table. Move it to §Open Questions as a named entry obligation blocked on the REQ erratum emitted with this review (G-1's "no configuration change is required" versus AC-5.1a's "or the configuration section is absent"). If REQ resolves toward G-1, §I.2's `present` gate, the §I.2 divergence table's last row and §I.4's "disabled run executes no new code at all" reasoning all change; if it resolves toward AC-5.1a, say in §I.2 that G-1 is knowingly not met on an unconfigured repository so the trade is visible to the operator rather than buried in a design table.
2. **F-02 (Medium)** — Add C-1's `docType` conjunct at the attachment point, or state the coincidence as an invariant in §A.2 and give `learningsDispatchSet.test.js` a set-equality assertion over the `docType`s that reach the injector.
3. **F-03 (Medium)** — Name the instrument that carries AC-5.2's *write* half in §T.6, and scope the seam call log to the read half explicitly.
4. **F-04 (Medium)** — State how C-8's "less is injected" half is discharged (static caps only), and carry it to REQ O-1 / T-O-3 with a stated consequence if the measurement contradicts it.
5. **F-05, F-06 (Low)** — Byte-order comparator or a recorded ASCII assumption in §D.4; "six" → "seven" in §Scope.
