## Positive Observations

- **The traceability table is total over the REQ.** All 26 of REQ v0.10's acceptance criteria have a row in §9, each with a named red task and named green task(s) — I checked set-equality against the REQ, not containment, and there are no extras and no gaps. That is the single most useful property a PLAN can have from this lens.
- **The citations are, with one exception, exact.** Every `BL-PREREQ` line number in §2 resolves at HEAD (`adapter.mjs:215/:161/:116/:75`, `transport.mjs:135/:89/:23/:33/:46/:55`, `startup.mjs:60/:145/:20`, `report.mjs:36/:70`, `run.mjs:52/:58/:80/:114`, `skills.mjs:204/:267/:290/:312`, `handshake.mjs:137/:183/:20/:86`), and so do `orchestrate-dev.js:6229-6231` — which really is exactly the three-entry reviewer-role `MAP`, so T07's "asserted as **exactly**" is a claim the code supports.
- **Errata raised rather than absorbed.** Both are real and I confirmed both independently: TSPEC §8.3's edit surface does list `pr-tests.yml` and omits `.claude/pdlc.config.json`; and `RungRecord = { rung: 0..5 }` (`TSPEC:840`) genuinely cannot represent FSPEC v1.6's inserted rung 4a (`FSPEC:299`, `:406-407`). Choosing FSPEC as the authority for observable behaviour and routing the gap upstream is the right call, and §9's paragraph explains it in terms an operator can act on.
- **The vacuous-green failure mode is scheduled against, not noted.** §1's point 1 and §10's second risk row both name `observed ⊆ OUTCOMES` passing over the empty set, and the emptiness guard lands with T19 rather than as later hardening. This is the failure mode that would let the whole suite-wide property layer ship green and worthless; putting the spine at batch 3 and its self-test at batch 2 is the fix that actually holds.
- **AC-1.5's anti-fork obligations are carried into commands.** §11's `git diff --stat $(git merge-base HEAD main)..HEAD -- pdlc/workflows/` and `git status --porcelain .claude/workflows/` turn C-4 and the untracked-consumer-copy rule into two lines a reviewer can run, including the observation that `--check` compares content and cannot see tracking state.

## Recommendation

**Needs revision** — two High findings.

Exactly what to change, in order:

1. **F-01** — add `AT-ENG-02`, `05`, `17`, `18`, `32`, `40`, `57`, `66` to the owning tasks and to §9's AT column; restore AC-1.3's range to `AT-ENG-52…AT-ENG-57`. If any of the eight is deliberately out of scope, say so in §10 with the reason, so the omission is a decision on the record rather than a gap.

3. **F-03** — regenerate §9's AT column from FSPEC §14.1; mark additions as additions.
4. **F-04** — move T10 into AC-1.5's green column and reword the cell to name clause (a) vs clause (b).
5. **F-05** — correct §11's job table to `script-syntax` (`:161`), `unit-tests` (`:27`), `fresh-clone-bootstrap` (`:103`), `npm ci` (`:68`) / `npm test` (`:75`).
6. **F-06**, **F-07** — one clause each: name v(a)/v(b)'s fixture content in T48; say where AC-6.2's live evidence is recorded.

Nothing in the batch order, the ownership manifest or the dependency argument needs to change — the schedule is sound and the reasoning behind it is the strongest part of the document. Every finding above is a traceability or scoping edit to §8, §9 and §11.
