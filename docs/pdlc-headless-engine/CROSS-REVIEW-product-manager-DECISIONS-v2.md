| F-01 | Medium | Local | **The containment guard's four-position scoping *is* an exemption list, so §8's "no exemption list" overstates it.** DEC-ENG-05 replaces the closed allow-list with containment, and the wording is scoped to four positions: "at a dispatch call site, in a `PHASE_DISPATCH` role field, in a `skill:` field, or bound to a module-local constant". That scoping is load-bearing, not decorative — a skill-identifier-shaped literal outside those four positions exists at HEAD: `meta.name = "orchestrate-dev"` (`pdlc/workflows/orchestrate-dev.js:3316`) and `meta.name = "orchestrate-queue"` (`pdlc/workflows/orchestrate-queue.js:45`). The consequence for the claim that follows: "it fails exactly when the drift this decision fears occurs — a new dispatch site naming an identifier the exports do not carry" holds only if the new site uses one of the four idioms. A new idiom (an array of identifiers, a template literal, a `role:`-style field) escapes silently, which is the same class of hole the closed allow-list had, relocated from names to positions. §8's row says "no exemption list" without that qualification. Fix (one paragraph, no decision reversed): state the position enumeration as the guard's known boundary, name `meta.name` as the reason it is positional rather than global, and make "the enumeration grows" a re-evaluation trigger alongside the two already listed. | REQ AC-3.5 (`REQ:499-516`); FSPEC BR-START-4 |
| F-02 | Medium | Local | **DEC-ENG-03's retained "narrow half" still asserts one operator-visible behaviour whose channel is upstream's to create.** The entry keeps as decided: "the probe's observation is **reported at startup** rather than discovered per dispatch", while deferring to upstream "whether the observation refuses the run or reports as a failed rung". Fix: reduce the retained half to what is genuinely DECISIONS-local — *the engine does not rely on the guard's fail-open branch, and observes interpreter availability once at startup rather than per dispatch* — and put the surfacing (refusal, rung, or report line) wholly on the upstream side, where the two deferred items already sit. | FSPEC BR-START-2; TSPEC §6.4 |

DEC-ENG-03's split into "the engineering position" and "where its authority has to come from" is the cleanest possible resolution of F-01: the judgement survives intact and legible, only its home changes.
- **F-06 was over-delivered rather than patched.** I asked for a two-name→three-name correction; the author re-measured HEAD properly and found five, enumerated eleven call sites with line numbers, and flagged the multi-line call forms that defeat a single-line grep. A DECISIONS document that records its own corrected mistakes is worth more to the next reader than one that quietly reads correct, because it shows which claims were checked.
