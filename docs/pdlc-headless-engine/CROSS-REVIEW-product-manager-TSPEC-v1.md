## Positive Observations

- **§7.3 is the strongest section in the document.** It names the exact way a parity oracle goes vacuous (a double that returns a string and writes nothing), fixes the double to replay creation events, forbids it from writing the approval anchors so the clause tests `orchestrate-dev.js:6190`'s own append logic (verified), and asserts the negative half **first** (AT-ENG-45). That is the repo's `consolidation-agent-vacuous-green` lesson applied without being asked.
- **§6.5 refuses to pretend a measurement was made.** Declaring `DEFAULT_PERMISSION_MODE = "bypassPermissions"` (`transport.mjs:89`, verified) unmeasured against PreToolUse deny, and pre-committing both consequence branches so a red result is not a design debate, is exactly how a C-9/DC-02 obligation should be handed to PLAN.
- **§3.2 makes AC-2.1 row 5 fixturable without an operator credential**, which is what the REQ's Phase-F erratum was reaching for: `resolveAuthPosture` pure over injected `env`/`evidence`, `HOME` pointed at a scratch tree. Every row of the first-match list becomes testable, and the ordered-list totality (row 6) is preserved.
- **§5.3 refuses to fabricate a pipeline outcome.** Engine-fatal stops write no POSTMORTEM and touch no queue row, on the stated ground that an invented POSTMORTEM would be indistinguishable from one the review loop produced — which is precisely what the repo's `RESOLVED: yes` halt-clearing protocol depends on. Product-correct, and reasoned from the consequence rather than the convenience.
- **§3.3 puts the dispatchable-skill tie on the workflows side, in a test, not in production code**, so a dispatch added without its export fails in this repo before the engine ever sees it — and it checks bundle-safety against `stripModuleSyntax` (`build-runtime.mjs:45`, verified). AC-3.5's "derived from the modules, never from a list the engine maintains" is honoured in mechanism, not just in prose.

## Recommendation

**Needs revision.** Four High findings, all of the same shape: an acceptance criterion whose obligation the design does not carry, or carries under a different subject. In priority order:

1. **F-04** — give AC-1.2 an owning section (the filesystem-observation method, the queue posture, the empty-set assertion), and correct §8.1's row, which currently describes a write oracle for a read criterion.
2. **F-03** — add the model-map row to §7.4's set-equality harness; pass-through alone cannot fail a phase that stops pinning.
3. **F-02** — enumerate §4.5's `engine` block against FSPEC §12.2's nine rows; today the startup auth id, per-phase counts, non-pause retry rows, tunables and permission posture are missing or renamed.
4. **F-01** — add the loop stop-reason field and outcome line; two terminations that both exit `0` are not distinguishable.

Then F-05 (repoint AC-1.5 at §2.4), F-06 (name `queue.maxIterations` and `dispatch.retryBackoff`, and reconcile the unbounded default against `run.mjs:273`), F-07 (one sentence).

Nothing here is a scope-creep finding: the design adds no product behaviour the REQ does not ask for, and §9.2's three raised questions are correctly raised rather than silently decided. The gaps are all omissions, and every one of them is closable inside §§4.5, 7.4, 8.1 plus one new subsection.
