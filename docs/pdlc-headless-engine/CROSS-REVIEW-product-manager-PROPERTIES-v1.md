## Positive Observations

- The HEAD column is genuinely grounded, not decorative. Every anchor I spot-checked resolves and says what the row claims: `runQueueLoop`'s `maxPasses = 100` default (`pdlc/engine/lib/run.mjs:273`, PROP-QUEUE-5/15), `Infinity` on the flag-absent CLI path (`bin/pdlc.mjs:305`), the literal `transport: "agent-sdk"` (`lib/report.mjs:50`, PROP-REP-10), `DEFAULT_PERMISSION_MODE` (`lib/transport.mjs:89`), `DEFAULT_TIMEOUT_MS` (`:64`), the scalar `lastApiKeySource` (`lib/adapter.mjs:245`, PROP-REP-8), `composeDispatchPrompt` (`lib/skills.mjs:312`). The three "does not exist at HEAD" claims are also true: `lib/auth.mjs` and `lib/outcome.mjs` are absent from `pdlc/engine/lib/`, and `M-ENG-09` has no row in `docs/_constraints/pdlc-engine-baseline.md` (PROP-GUARD-20). A reviewer can open any cell and check it — that is the property a HEAD column is for.
- §12's discipline is the strongest part of the document. Requiring every must-not to name the positive it is paired with **in the same test file**, in a column of the table rather than in prose, makes the absence-only failure mode structurally hard rather than merely discouraged. NEG-13 (guard denies / guard allows), NEG-4 (`{"none"}` proceeds / flag widens) and NEG-19 (deep-equal plus key-set difference of exactly one) are the right shape.
- §16.2 is an honest gap table rather than a defensive one. G-PROP-1 stating plainly that no hermetic property proves the SDK actually feeds a `PreToolUse` deny back under `bypassPermissions` — the feature's largest safety question — and routing it to a credentialed live run scheduled *before unattended use*, is exactly the disclosure a product owner needs to decide whether to turn this on.
- The set-equality rows resist the usual one-direction shortcut and say where each direction is observed. PROP-FAIL-2/3 splitting forward (suite-wide accumulation) from reverse (six named provocation fixtures), and PROP-MODEL-7 refusing to loosen the oracle when a map row is unreachable in the corpus, keep the AC-4.1 and AC-3.3 promises intact rather than restating them weakly.
