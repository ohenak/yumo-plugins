## Positive Observations

- REQ-DECLEDGER-02 is the strongest AC here: byte-identity against a *committed* baseline, explicitly ruling out a same-branch before/after assertion (C-2), is a positive-equality oracle rather than an absence-only one, and it is the shape the shipped `learningsBaselineGuard.test.js` precedent already proves runnable.
- REQ-DECLEDGER-05 is directly writable as a parameterised property over {three keys} x {wrong type, malformed, absent}, with the blast-radius assertion (other keys and other blocks unaffected) stated explicitly — the per-key fail-open precedent it cites is real and shipped (`REQ-LOOPECON-08`, `pdlc/workflows/__tests__/loopEconomicsConfig.test.js`).
- REQ-DECLEDGER-01's freshness clause is testable as written: mutate a decision record between two dispatches in one round window and assert the second dispatch reflects the mutation. That is a positive, falsifiable oracle, not the absence-shaped "no stale snapshot" it could easily have been.
- C-5 declares thresholds with defaults, types and owners rather than leaving TSPEC to invent them, and R-4 honestly labels them as unmeasured author defaults — the assumption is visible and vetoable instead of buried.
- Every project-level and cross-feature citation I checked resolves and says what the REQ claims: `DEC-ERRROUTE-01` (`docs/_decisions/DECISIONS-erratum-routing.md:12`), `DEC-TERM-01`/`DEC-TERM-02` (`docs/_decisions/DECISIONS-loop-termination.md:11,28`), the proposal's M4/R3-2 rows and its 114-approving-verdicts and shared-dedupe-key claims (`docs/design/PROPOSAL-pdlc-pipeline-optimization-2026-08-27.html`). No nonexistent-authority citation in this document.

## Recommendation

**Needs revision**

Five High findings. Four of them (F-01, F-02, F-03, F-04) share one root: the document's central mechanism is described in terms whose expected values cannot be derived from anything that exists on HEAD — a record field that no decision record carries, a per-finding accounting channel that ordinary rounds do not have, an evidence-novelty predicate with no rule, and a relevance set with no definition.
