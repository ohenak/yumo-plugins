| F-01 | Medium | Cross-Feature | **T-21 promotes an over-broad co-change rule into a standing project constraint.** The Overview's premise (PLAN §Overview, "The standing cost this PLAN has to carry") states that "Adding one module to `pdlc/workflows/lib/` obliges a co-change across the vendoring enumerations", and T-21 promotes that sweep to `docs/_constraints/DOMAIN-CONSTRAINTS.md`, where it binds every future feature. The premise is false as stated: `pdlc/workflows/lib/document-oracles.mjs` exists at HEAD and appears in **none** of the five enumerations — not `prepack.mjs`'s `MODULE_NAMES`, neither `WORKFLOW_MEMBERS` copy, not `fixture-machine.mjs`'s `WORKFLOW_MODULE_NAMES`, not `c8.include` — because it is a dev-only oracle helper consumed solely by `documentOracles.test.js` and `advisoryWaveGate.test.js`. The real trigger is **runtime-reachability from the shipped engine**, not membership of `lib/`. The conclusion for `stats.mjs` is unaffected (it *is* reachable through the engine CLI, so batch 10 is genuinely owed), and no task ordering needs to change — but the constraint text T-21 writes must be scoped to runtime-reachable modules, or the next dev-only `lib/` module is forced into the vendored payload it must not join, and a reviewer who applies the constraint literally will red a correct change. Fix: scope the promoted constraint by reachability and cite `document-oracles.mjs` as the worked exclusion. | REQ C-1, NG-4; `DEC-STATS-01` `K-9` |
