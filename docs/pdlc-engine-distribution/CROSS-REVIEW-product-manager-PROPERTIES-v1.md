## Positive Observations

- **Traceability is mechanically verifiable and it holds, in both directions.** I re-derived it rather than trusting the claim: all **30** REQ acceptance criteria (AC-1.1…AC-6.2) are cited; §4's table carries all **35** of FSPEC §8's `AT-` ids with set-equality in both directions and no strays; and every one of the **59** task ids cited resolves to a PLAN §2 row, with no PLAN task left uncited. For a 105-property catalogue spanning five work streams, that is unusually clean.
- **The oracle discipline is genuinely strong and is applied consistently, not decoratively.** §3's seventeen negative properties each carry a named positive conjunct, and the pattern recurs where it matters most: PROP-INSTALL-2's positive control proving the recorder observes writes at all, PROP-PROV-2's baseline fixture asserted non-empty before byte-identity, PROP-PACK-5 running `prepack` *first* because "every entry in the manifest…" is vacuously true over an empty set. These are the exact failure modes a reviewer usually has to raise; here they are pre-empted in the document's own words.
- **The declared gaps are stated honestly, in product terms, with their cost named.** §5's "AC-4.4 is verified once and never again — a hardcoded pair introduced later by T20/T27/T29/… would redden **no test in this plan**" is the sentence a PM most needs and least often gets. Recording the condition under which the exclusion expires (the moment a revert leg exists, move PROP-PROV-19 onto T50) turns a standing carve-out into a scheduling note, which is the right shape.
- **The preservation floors are accurate against HEAD.** I checked each: `engine-config.test.js` 9 sites, `run.test.js` 21, `seam-contract.test.js` 12, `skills-composition.test.js` 14 sites → 32 executed. All four match the stated numbers exactly. F-05 is the single exception, and it is an under-application of the document's own rule rather than a wrong number.
- **Scope is respected.** No property invents product behaviour absent from REQ/FSPEC, and the expected-set ownership rule in §1 (member names from TSPEC §5.4, classes and per-class counts from FSPEC §5.2) correctly implements REQ v0.11's AC-1.3 split rather than re-deciding it.

## Recommendation

**Needs revision** — two High findings, both concentrated in the same place: the `AT-1.*` family.

The document is strong on oracle quality and traceability mechanics. What F-01 and F-02 expose is narrower and fixable: the properties covering REQ-EDIST-01's refusal path assert the **launcher/engine-store** half (`store.empty`, pins, config) while the criteria they are mapped to are about the **plugin compat handshake** (declared range, "none installed", out-of-range plugin). The underlying behaviour is not missing — it is shipped and tested at `handshake.test.js:110,120` — so this is a mapping and coverage-claim defect, not a hole in the product. But it currently lets §5 report REQ-EDIST-01 as completely carried when G-1's central promise has no property asserting it.

Exactly what to change:

1. **F-01** — give AT-1.1/AT-1.2/AT-1.3 a carrier that asserts the compat refusal (naming `handshake.test.js`, and transcribing the message conjuncts: declared range, "none installed" / version found, no project file changed), or re-point those three rows and let PROP-LAUNCH-1/-2/-4 claim only the launcher-half ids they assert. Then re-check §5's "all six `AT-1.*` ids carried".
2. **F-02** — restate PROP-LAUNCH-2 against an installed **plugin** version outside the declared range, not a pin; pins are engine versions (T-5) and are never checked against `pdlcPluginCompat` (T-3).
3. **F-03** — add per-member conjuncts to PROP-LAUNCH-5 so AC-1.4's three named fields are pinned, not just mutually equal.
4. **F-04** — state the disposition of the seven HEAD test files pinning the `REMEDY` text that PROP-VER-14 rewrites.
5. **F-05** — give `ci-arrangement.test.js` the dual floor (≥ 6 executed from ≥ 2 sites) for the reason PROP-REGR-1 already states for `skills-composition.test.js`.

None of these requires re-opening a settled decision, and none touches the task graph, the ownership manifest or §4's set-equality. F-01 and F-02 are edits to three property rows; F-03/F-04/F-05 are one clause each.
