## Positive Observations

- **§2 is real measurement, not restated prose.** I re-read every row I could check — V-01 through V-21 — and the citations hold: the three O-8 blockers (`pdlc/engine/package.json:2,4,11`), the twelve `lib/*.mjs`, the relative escape at `lib/run.mjs:51-55`, the walk and `PROP-FORK-1` at `__tests__/run.test.js:51-79`, `readPluginVersion`/`checkCompat`/`REMEDY` at `lib/handshake.mjs:45,144,131`, `versions` at `lib/startup.mjs:453`, `resolvePluginRoot`'s explicit branch at `lib/skills.mjs:204-231`, and `ENGINE_CONFIG_PATH` at `lib/run.mjs:160`. That is a rare baseline to review against, and it is what let me disagree with §7.4 concretely rather than by suspicion.
- **§12.3 is the right instinct, applied by name.** Calling out three oracles that must not be satisfiable by absence, and pairing each with a positive (the four-kind halted fixture, the per-leg install positives, the literal packed list), is exactly the discipline PROPERTIES will need. F-07 is a fourth one that belongs in that list, not a rejection of the section.
- **P-1's default-inert seam with a named precedent** (`advisoryDisabled.test.js`, PROP-DIS-*) means the byte-identity proof already has a shipped template, and §12.4 sequences it before any placement. That ordering is correct and worth keeping verbatim.
- **§5.4's asymmetry argument** — prefer the failure caught offline over the one caught by a consumer — is the correct testing rationale for an allow-list, independent of F-04's arithmetic problem with its members.
- **The test-doubles table (§12.2) justifies each double against the real thing**, including the one that matters most: rehearsing AT-3.3 against the real registry would burn a version irreversibly and leave the no-op branch unobservable.

## Recommendation

**Needs revision** — seven High findings.

The design is well-grounded and the review below is mostly arithmetic against sets the TSPEC
itself chose to be checked by equality. The blocking ones cluster in three places: the
provenance placement set is short of AC-5.3's fourth kind and §7.4's measurement of
`artifactPaths` does not hold (F-01, F-02); the package's declared contents disagree with the
expected set and cannot carry the install mechanism §9 depends on (F-03, F-04); and two oracles
fail open where they are load-bearing (F-05's `uses:` blind spot, F-07's empty-manifest AF-2),
with F-06 leaving a corrupt config silently resolving to "no pin".

Concretely, to reach approval: state the single marked commit helper and the kind-3 carrier;
replace §7.4's claim with the literal authored-class enumeration AT-4.5 needs; reconcile the
`files` list with FSPEC §5.2 (erratum or drop `README.md`) and give `postinstall` a packed home;
add the `uses:`-is-unexpandable rule to §8.5; give AF-2 a prepack precondition, a set-equality
and a mutation falsifier; and add the unreadable-config ladder row with a three-way reader.
