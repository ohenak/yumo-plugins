## Positive Observations

- The compat-range handshake is the right shape, and it is not speculative: `pdlc/engine/lib/handshake.mjs:80-130` already implements a deliberately closed range grammar (`^`, `~`, exact, `*`) with an explicit "anything else is a hard error, never a silent pass" stance, and `:134`'s remedy string already names the operator's way out. AC-1.1 and AC-3.7 are gating the mechanism that exists, not one that has to be invented.
- AC-1.3's "no `skills/` directory or prompt file of any kind, decidable from the package's own contents without network access" is exactly the right kind of criterion: an absence assertion that is decidable locally and paired with AC-1.2's positive assertion about where prompts *do* come from. That pairing is what keeps it from being an absence-only oracle.
- R-3 is the most valuable paragraph in the document. It names the residual skew axis the design change *accepts* — identical engine+plugin pairs can still have executed different `SKILL.md` text — and explicitly declines to claim any AC closes it. Stating a known-open risk honestly is worth more than a criterion that pretends to close it.
- AC-3.2's "a skipped or green-but-inert run is a defect, because it is indistinguishable from success" is precisely the failure mode CI gates die of, called out at REQ level where it can still shape the workflow's design.
- The obligation section shows its work: O-2 and O-5 are answered against cited prior decisions (DEC-HE-02) and cited shipped code, and O-5 goes as far as naming a real unresolved tension (`PDLC_PLUGIN_ROOT` honored on presence alone vs T-6's "never inferred") instead of declaring the obligation closed. That is the correct way to hand an open question to TSPEC.

## Recommendation

**Needs revision**

Five High findings. The three that must move before FSPEC authoring begins: BL-03's version-of-record
prerequisite is unresolved yet the REQ declares no obligation open (F-01); §1.1's O-F and O-B are
stale against HEAD in ways that mis-size REQ-EDIST-04 and under-gate AC-3.4 (F-02, F-04); and the
package cannot physically contain the workflow modules as AC-1.3 states without a packaging decision
that collides with an upstream green test (F-05). F-03 is a one-line fix but belongs in O-8 so the
publish preconditions are a complete set.

Concretely, the next revision should: add an obligation resolving BL-03 (or split T-1 into two named
values); re-measure §1.1 at HEAD, moving the code-level facts into `docs/_constraints/` as `M-*` ids
and citing them; restate T-7/AC-3.4 as set-equality over the live required-check set; add a §6 risk
for the module-packaging/anti-fork collision; fold `"private": true` into O-8; and reconcile AC-2.1
with AC-1.1 by naming AC-2.1's observable pass condition.
