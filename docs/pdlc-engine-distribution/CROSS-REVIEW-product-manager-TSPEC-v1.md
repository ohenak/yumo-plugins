## Positive Observations

- **§2's verified baseline is the strongest part of the document, and it changes the plan rather than decorating it.** Every row I spot-checked held: V-01's four manifest fields (`pdlc/engine/package.json:2,3,4,10,11`), V-03's exactly twelve `lib/*.mjs`, V-04's relative escape (`run.mjs:52-55`), V-05's two-assertion anti-fork oracle (`run.test.js:51-79`), V-14's `artifactPaths` (`orchestrate-dev.js:11659`, `:11507`, `:13088`), V-18's five job-level names (`pr-tests.yml:28,78,112,138,196` on `os: [ubuntu-latest] / node: ['20']`), V-21's DEC-DIST-01…05 with no version-of-record entry. Measuring first is why §7 could shrink O-9 from three unknowns to one.
- **§7.4 unblocks AT-4.5 on evidence and raises the erratum rather than silently re-scoping.** The FSPEC marked it `[blocked on O-9]`; the TSPEC proves the carrier already ships and says it is raising an erratum. That is exactly the right handling of an upstream claim that measurement contradicts.
- **§7.3 refuses to dress the interim state as a solution.** AC-6.2's bundle-side load root is left open by name, re-opened in §14.3 N-1, and the limit is pushed into the test itself per Q-2. Honest residue beats a fixture that distinguishes nothing.
- **§5.3's anti-fork restatement satisfies BR-8.2's hardest clause.** AF-1's tracked-ness test carries exactly the "vendored in the repo vs. vendored in a build artefact" distinction BR-8.2 demanded, AF-2 asserts the property the old walk was a proxy for, and §14.2's R-A states the one scenario the restatement is weaker against so a reviewer can disagree with it explicitly. No oracle deleted without a named stronger replacement.
- **§13 covers every acceptance criterion in the REQ**, and §4.2's derivation of the version store from AC-5.1 + AC-5.5 is the right shape of argument: the criteria force the infrastructure, so K-2 is priced as a requirement rather than defended as an ambition.

## Recommendation

**Needs revision** — four High findings.

This is a strong document: it measures before it decides, it takes all five parked obligations
with real rejected alternatives, and it is honest about the one it cannot close. The revision is
bounded and none of it re-opens a settled decision. Exactly four things to change:

1. **F-01** — reconcile §5.4's `files` allow-list with FSPEC §5.2's expected set, including npm's
   unconditional `README`/`LICENSE` inclusion. Today PF-4 and AT-3.8a fail on the first run.
2. **F-02** — give AC-5.3's four kinds four placements in §7.2 P-3, or raise an erratum against
   kind 4. §7.2 and §13's AC-5.3 row currently disagree.
3. **F-03** — either make §8.2's fallback (duplicate job bodies) the primary, or extend §8.5's
   rendering rule to model `uses:` nesting *before* the extraction task is scheduled. The named
   mitigation cannot currently detect the failure it is offered against, and the blast radius is a
   live consumer pipeline's Phase PUB poll.
4. **F-04** — carry the `doctor`/`--version` exemption through the new launcher-side gate in §6.2
   and §11, and say what triple they report when the store is empty.

The three Medium findings (F-05 scope name, F-06 probe default, F-07 AC-2.3's observed value) and
the two Low ones are worth folding into the same pass but do not gate on their own.
